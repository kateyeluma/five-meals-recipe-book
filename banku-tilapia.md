# Banku and Tilapia

## Description
Banku and Tilapia is a popular Ghanaian meal made with fermented corn and cassava dough, served with grilled tilapia and pepper sauce.

## Ingredients

### For the Banku
- Corn dough
- Cassava dough
- Water
- Salt

### For the Tilapia
- Fresh tilapia
- Onion
- Garlic
- Ginger
- Pepper
- Salt
- Cooking oil

## Preparation

### Making the Banku
1. Mix the corn dough and cassava dough with water.
2. Pour the mixture into a cooking pot.
3. Stir continuously over medium heat.
4. Add water if necessary and continue stirring until firm and smooth.
5. Shape and serve.

### Preparing the Tilapia
1. Clean and wash the fish.
2. Blend or crush the onion, garlic, ginger, pepper and salt.
3. Rub the seasoning over the fish.
4. Grill until properly cooked.

## Serving
Serve the banku with grilled tilapia and fresh pepper sauce.
