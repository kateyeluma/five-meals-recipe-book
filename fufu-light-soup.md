# Fufu and Light Soup

## Description
Fufu and Light Soup is a popular Ghanaian meal. Fufu is made from boiled cassava and plantain or yam, while light soup is a spicy tomato-based soup.

## Ingredients

### For the Fufu
- Cassava
- Plantain or yam
- Water

### For the Light Soup
- Fresh tomatoes
- Onion
- Pepper
- Fresh fish or meat
- Garlic
- Ginger
- Salt
- Water

## Preparation

### Making the Fufu
1. Peel and wash the cassava and plantain.
2. Cut them into pieces.
3. Boil until soft.
4. Pound or mash until smooth and stretchy.
5. Shape into portions.

### Making the Light Soup
1. Wash the tomatoes, onions and pepper.
2. Boil them until soft.
3. Blend or mash the vegetables.
4. Season the fish or meat with garlic, ginger and salt.
5. Add the blended vegetables and water.
6. Simmer until the soup and fish or meat are properly cooked.

## Serving
Serve the fufu with hot light soup.
