1. Jollof Rice

Description: Jollof rice is a popular West African dish prepared with rice, tomatoes and spices.

Preparation time: 20 minutes

Cooking time: 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of rice
  • 4 fresh tomatoes
  • Tomato paste
  • 1 onion
  • Cooking oil
  • Salt
  • Seasoning cubes
  • Pepper
  • Water or stock

Equipment Needed

  • Cooking pot
  • Knife
  • Chopping board
  • Cooking spoon
  • Bowl

Preparation Procedures

  1. Wash the rice thoroughly and set aside.
  2. Blend the tomatoes, pepper and onion.
  3. Heat cooking oil in a pot.
  4. Add chopped onions and tomato paste.
  5. Add the blended tomato mixture and cook.
  6. Add seasoning, salt and water or stock.
  7. Add the washed rice and stir gently.
  8. Cover and cook until the rice is soft.
  9. Serve while hot.

Serving suggestions: Serve with chicken, fish or vegetables.

2. Fried Rice

Description: Fried rice is a delicious meal made with rice, vegetables, seasoning and other ingredients.

Preparation time: 20 minutes

Cooking time: 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of rice
  • Carrots
  • Green beans
  • Sweet corn
  • Green pepper
  • 1 onion
  • Cooking oil
  • Seasoning cubes
  • Salt
  • Chicken or vegetable stock

Equipment Needed

  • Cooking pot
  • Frying pan
  • Knife
  • Chopping board
  • Cooking spoon

Preparation Procedures

  1. Wash the rice thoroughly.
  2. Cook the rice until it is almost soft.
  3. Wash and chop the vegetables.
  4. Heat cooking oil in a frying pan.
  5. Add onions and stir gently.
  6. Add the chopped vegetables.
  7. Add seasoning and salt.
  8. Add the cooked rice and mix properly.
  9. Stir-fry until everything is well combined.
  10. Serve hot.

Serving suggestions: Serve with chicken, fish or fried eggs.

3. Eru and Garri

Description: Eru and Garri is a popular Cameroonian meal made with vegetables, palm oil and water fufu or garri.

Preparation time: 25 minutes

Cooking time: 45 minutes

Ingredients

  • Fresh eru leaves
  • Waterleaf
  • Red palm oil
  • Smoked fish
  • Meat
  • Crayfish
  • Pepper
  • Salt
  • Seasoning cubes
  • Garri

Equipment Needed

  • Cooking pot
  • Knife
  • Cooking spoon
  • Bowl
  • Strainer

Preparation Procedures

  1. Wash the eru and waterleaf properly.
  2. Cut the vegetables into smaller pieces.
  3. Wash and prepare the meat and fish.
  4. Cook the meat until tender.
  5. Add the waterleaf and allow it to cook.
  6. Add the eru leaves and stir.
  7. Add palm oil, crayfish and pepper.
  8. Add salt and seasoning to taste.
  9. Allow everything to cook properly.
  10. Prepare the garri according to its package instructions.
  11. Serve the eru with the prepared garri.

Serving suggestions: Serve hot with garri or water fufu.

4. Beans and Plantain

Description: Beans and plantain is a nutritious meal prepared with beans and ripe plantain.

Preparation time: 15 minutes

Cooking time: 60 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of beans
  • 3 ripe plantains
  • 1 onion
  • Red palm oil
  • Pepper
  • Salt
  • Seasoning cubes
  • Water

Equipment Needed

  • Cooking pot
  • Knife
  • Cooking spoon
  • Bowl
  • Frying pan

Preparation Procedures

  1. Sort the beans to remove unwanted particles.
  2. Wash the beans thoroughly.
  3. Put the beans in a pot with clean water.
  4. Cook until the beans become soft.
  5. Chop the onion and pepper.
  6. Add palm oil, onion, pepper and seasoning.
  7. Stir and allow the beans to cook for a few more minutes.
  8. Peel and slice the ripe plantains.
  9. Fry the plantain until golden and cooked through.
  10. Serve the beans with fried plantain.

Serving suggestions: Serve warm with fried ripe plantain.

5. Chicken Stew

Description: Chicken stew is a delicious sauce prepared with chicken, tomatoes, onions and spices.

Preparation time: 20 minutes

Cooking time: 45 minutes

Ingredients

  • Chicken pieces
  • 5 fresh tomatoes
  • Tomato paste
  • 2 onions
  • Cooking oil
  • Garlic
  • Ginger
  • Pepper
  • Salt
  • Seasoning cubes
  • Water

Equipment Needed

  • Cooking pot
  • Knife
  • Chopping board
  • Blender
  • Cooking spoon
  • Bowl

Preparation Procedures

  1. Wash the chicken properly and place it in a pot.
  2. Add salt, seasoning, onion and ginger.
  3. Cook the chicken until tender.
  4. Blend the tomatoes, pepper and garlic.
  5. Heat cooking oil in another pot.
  6. Add chopped onions and tomato paste.
  7. Add the blended tomato mixture.
  8. Allow the tomato mixture to cook properly.
  9. Add the cooked chicken and some stock.
  10. Add seasoning and adjust the salt.
  11. Allow the stew to simmer until well cooked.
  12. Serve hot.

Serving suggestions: Serve with rice, boiled yam or plantain.
