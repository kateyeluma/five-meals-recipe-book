# Waakye

## Description
Waakye is a popular Ghanaian meal made with rice and beans, traditionally cooked with dried millet leaves.

## Ingredients
- Rice
- Beans
- Dried millet leaves (waakye leaves)
- Water
- Salt

## Preparation
1. Pick and wash the beans.
2. Boil the beans until they are partly soft.
3. Wash the millet leaves and add them to the pot.
4. Add the rice and enough water.
5. Add salt and stir gently.
6. Cook until the rice and beans are soft.
7. Serve hot with stew, spaghetti, or boiled eggs.

## Serving
Serve warm with your preferred side dishes.
